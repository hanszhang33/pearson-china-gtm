Appendix B — Outbound-intel screening methodology (50-account system)

Purpose: the complete, repeatable screening-and-operations system behind the 50-account engine (Versant assessment + Mondly training, ¥3m+ deals). It consolidates five prior files into one executable framework. Companion data: outbound-intel.csv (master, 14 cols × 50 rows) / outbound-intel.xlsx (visual filter) / outbound-intel-log.md (weekly archive).

Core assumption: outbound = language-capability need = Pearson entry point. Every filter runs on three hard metrics: overseas-revenue share / overseas-volume target / large-scale overseas hiring with an English bar.

§1 Screening logic (how the 50 were chosen)

Entry conditions (any one qualifies):

1. High overseas-revenue share (≥40%, or absolute ≥¥10bn)

2. Top-tier overseas volume/exports (e.g. top-10 vehicle exports, tier-1 module shipments)

3. Large-scale overseas employment: overseas plants / subsidiaries / project teams hiring English-as-working-language

4. Active overseas M&A / plant-build / JV (cross-cultural integration need)

5. Overseas digital 0→1 (cross-border e-com, overseas warehouse, overseas App)

Industry pool (distribution of 50): Medical/Pharma/CXO 10 · New-energy/Auto/Battery 9 · Automotive 7 · Consumer-electronics/Internet/E-com 11 · Construction machinery 4 · Home appliances 4 · Infrastructure 4 · Chemicals 1.

Exclusion: no real outbound business/plan; sub-scale workforce.

§3 Weekly signal monitoring (Monday automation)

Four signal types: ① new outbound move / overseas-revenue disclosure · ② executive or HR-lead change · ③ large-scale overseas-role hiring · ④ overseas project / bid / order. Process: read the 50-list → WebSearch each (company + "overseas/international" + last-week keywords) → record signal/implication/next-step → update csv + xlsx + weekly log. ~30 WebSearches per Monday.

§4 Decision-maker mining (find the person who approves ¥3m)

Maimai is for contacting names, not finding them. Real names live in public sources: ① Qichacha/Tianyancha "key personnel" · ② annual-report/exe pages (Eastmoney/Sina) · ③ HR media (HRflag/LatePost) · ④ Liepin talent pools · ⑤ paid signals (Tanji/Liaoxiao/Yixia) · ⑥ co-sell partners (Beisen/Mercer/WTW/KornFerry/Moka) for warm CHRO intros. Already-identified named decision-makers (public, from 2026-07): Mindray (Li Zaiwen, SVP Global Marketing), BYD (Stella Li), Chery (Zhang Guibing, GM International), Haier (Dr Yu Wentao, Int'l CHRO), ByteDance (Hua Wei/Ronnie, HR lead).

§5 Language-readiness diagnostics (the hook: v2 depth / v3 consulting)

A free "outbound talent language-readiness" health-check built from public, verifiable data — used as the first-touch hook. Principles: every count from sampled JDs/public disclosures (sample 15–30 int'l roles); every scorecard dimension cites evidence; every source lists a URL. v3 structure (8 parts): Exec summary · Methodology · Target portrait · Language-need map · LRI scorecard (10 dims × 1–5, AHP-weighted) · Peer benchmark · Risk quantification (gap→money, scenario + ROI) · Soft CTA. Completed: Mindray (v2+v3), BYD/Haier (v3); pending P0: CATL/Chery/Transsion/ByteDance/SHEIN/Midea.
